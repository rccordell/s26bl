## April 24: Collaborative Prints

_Lab Prep_: Our print labs will involve potential messes of several kinds, including ink, oils and solvents, and simple dirt. We do have protective gear such as aprons and gloves, but even so I would not recommend wearing your best linen suit; please wear something that you won't mind getting dirty.
